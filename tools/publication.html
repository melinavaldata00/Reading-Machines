Reading Machines
Publication
about

A book that can be read from two directions. From one side, it's made for you — words arranged the way books usually arrange them, meant to be read, understood, remembered. Turned upside down, the same pages show what's left after a machine read the same text.

Reading Machines began with a simple question: what happens when reading becomes a computational process, and how can graphic design engage with it? We rely on machines to read and produce text without really knowing what they're doing, as if they understood it the way we do — this book doesn't take that for granted.

You can flip through both readings at any time.
